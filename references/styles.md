# Krea 2 Style Catalog (Curated)

A curated set of mediums and genres Krea 2 handles well, with trigger phrasing and
example lines. Use these when the user wants a specific look. Lead the prompt with
the **medium/style phrase**, then layer the five-attribute stack
(see `best-practices.md`). Pick one style per prompt — stacking two fights for the
same latent budget and produces mush.

Each entry gives: the **trigger phrase** to lead with, the **key cues** that sell
it, and one **example** line.

## Photography

**35mm film** — trigger: "35mm film photograph". Cues: fine grain, shallow depth
of field, soft natural light, named muted palette.
Example: *a lone red fox on a misty hillside at dawn, 35mm film photograph, shallow depth of field, fine grain, soft backlight through the fog, muted green and amber palette.*

**Macro** — trigger: "macro photograph". Cues: extreme proximity, single sharp
focal point, pitch-black or solid background, dramatic light.
Example: *macro photograph, a glass of iced coffee on a marble counter, hard directional window light, deep shadows, condensation droplets, shallow depth of field, solid dark background.*

**Medium-format portrait** — trigger: "medium format portrait". Cues: creamy
tonal transitions, shallow DOF, controlled studio or window light.

**Disposable-flash** — trigger: "disposable camera flash photo". Cues: harsh
on-camera flash, blown highlights, snapshot framing.

**Long-exposure night** — trigger: "long-exposure night photograph". Cues: light
trails, motion blur on one element, deep shadows.

**Golden hour** — trigger: "golden hour photograph". Cues: low raking light, long
soft shadows, warm low-contrast glow.

**Cinematic** — trigger: "cinematic photography" (anchor to a cinematographer or
film for control). Cues: wide aspect feel, motivated lighting, negative space.
Example: *cinematic portrait photography, studio shot, model with head tilted back, wet-look black hair, dramatic lighting, large defocused sphere background, soft focus.*

## Anime & cel

**Anime cel-shading** — trigger: "anime illustration, cel shading". Cues: one
strong practical light source, flat shading, reflective surface, two-color palette.
Example: *anime illustration, a girl in a yellow raincoat at a rainy train platform at night, cel shading, glowing vending machine light, reflections on wet concrete, soft blue and warm yellow palette, wide shot.*

**Ligne claire** — trigger: "ligne claire illustration". Cues: clean uniform
outlines, flat color, no rendering noise.

## Print & graphic

**Risograph** — trigger: "risograph print". Cues: 2–3 color limited palette,
slight misregistration, grainy ink texture, bold flat silhouettes, solid bg.
Example: *risograph print, a bicycle leaning against a lamppost, two-color limited palette of fluorescent pink and navy, bold flat silhouettes, slight misregistration, grainy ink texture, solid cream background.*

**Screen print / linocut** — trigger: "linocut print" or "screen print". Cues:
bold cut shapes, ink bleed or chatter marks, limited palette.

**Ukiyo-e woodblock** — trigger: "ukiyo-e woodblock print". Cues: flat planes,
outlined forms, traditional palette.

**Flat vector / pop art** — trigger: "flat 2d vector illustration". Cues: bold
black outlines, high-saturation flat colors, geometric shapes, solid chroma bg.
Example: *flat 2d vector illustration, a grey cat walking in profile, tail high, speeding yellow taxi with motion blur behind, solid bright chroma green background, bold black outlines, kinetic pop art style.*

## Painting

**Gouache** — trigger: "gouache painting". Cues: matte chalky surface, thick
visible brushstrokes, textured paper, loose painterly edges.
Example: *gouache painting, a fishing village at golden hour, thick visible brushstrokes, textured paper, warm ochre and teal palette, boats pulled up on the sand, loose painterly edges.*

**Impasto oil** — trigger: "impasto oil painting". Cues: heavy raised paint,
palette-knife texture, visible strokes.

**Watercolor** — trigger: "children's book watercolor" or "ink wash". Cues: soft
bleeds, wet edges, light washes.

**Pointillist** — trigger: "pointillist painting". Cues: tiny stippled dots of
complementary color, radiant textured surface.

## 3D & craft

**Stylized 3D render** — trigger: "stylized 3D render" / "3D rendered designer
toy". Cues: smooth matte or vinyl texture, studio lighting, solid vibrant bg.
Example: *stylized 3D toy character on a solid cobalt blue background, oversized head, large black eyes, textured blue sweater, chunky sneakers, smooth studio lighting, vibrant matte textures.*

**Claymation** — trigger: "claymation still". Cues: fingerprint texture, matte
clay surface, miniature-set lighting.

**Low-poly** — trigger: "low-poly 3D render". Cues: faceted geometry, flat
shading, simple palette.

## Lo-fi & degraded

Krea 2 takes degradation requests literally where most models quietly clean them
up — ask for the flaw by name.

**Grainy VHS still** — trigger: "extremely grainy lo-fi VHS still". Cues: tracking
lines, washed-out color, low dynamic range, motion blur on one element, named era.
Example: *extremely grainy lo-fi VHS still, a 1990s bedroom lit only by a CRT television showing static, tracking lines, washed-out color, low dynamic range, soft focus.*

**Y2K chrome** — trigger: "retro-futuristic Y2K cyber-aesthetic". Cues:
liquid-metal chrome, airbrush texture, starburst lens flares, gradient backdrop.

## Concept & illustration

**Fantasy concept art** — trigger: "fantasy concept art". Cues: flat graphic
design, solid backdrop, dynamic expressive composition, distorted proportions.
Example: *fantasy concept art of a dark blue skinned elven archer riding a white tiger, solid green backdrop, flat graphic design, distorted proportions, highly dynamic expressive composition.*

**Childlike drawing** — trigger: "childish crayon drawing". Cues: naive line,
waxy texture, simple subject.
Example: *childish crayon drawing, a dragon spraying a fire extinguisher at some fire.*
